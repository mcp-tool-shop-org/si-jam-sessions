The piano's samples

Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The host's piano plays the Salamander Grand Piano V3, a Yamaha C5 recorded by
Alexander Holm, in the FLAC edition FreePats assembled
(SalamanderGrandPiano-SFZ+FLAC-V3+20200602). The samples are licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence, whose legal code
follows this notice.

The samples are not part of the host and are not in its repository.
`host fetch-piano` downloads them from FreePats
(https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html) into the
user's own cache, after checking the archive against the SHA-256 the host
pins. The host decodes them, pitches them between sampled keys by
resampling, and plays them; it does not alter the files.

What the licence asks, and what the host does:
- Credit the original author and name the licence with its URI, reasonably
  to the medium (section 4(b)). The host prints the credit above whenever the
  piano plays (play, render, jam, preview), and every WAV it renders with the
  piano carries it in its LIST/INFO chunk, in the ICMT (comment) field.
- Keep the licence with the work (section 4(a)). This notice and the legal
  code travel with the host; `host notices` prints them.
- A performance or a recording made with the samples is an adaptation or a
  public performance under section 3; the same credit goes with it.
