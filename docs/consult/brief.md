What is being planned

si-jam-sessions is the super-intelligence counterpart to a shipped TypeScript product, ai-jam-sessions. It does not import that repository. The model may propose a musical action. A checker admits it. The score is the law. Heard audio is the picture.

The locked shape:

1. The law is Rust, edition 2024, rustc 1.98.1, one wasm cdylib with a raw C ABI. Status codes cross the boundary. Panics do not. The host passes bytes in. The law does not read the disk and does not own an audio device.
2. A score is integer ticks at one fixed pulses-per-quarter, normalized at ingest. Pitch, onset, duration, velocity, and voice are integers. The hashed snapshot is little-endian, versioned, sorted, and free of HashMap order. Replay is the seed plus the admitted-action log. The model is not called during replay.
3. Five verbs: ingest, place notes, label a chord, reharmonize a span, refuse with a reason. The reharmonization checker is a hand-authored predicate: melody locked, a consonance rule, a non-triviality fraction signed before any training run.
4. A song is ingested only when the composition is public domain in the US and the EU, with years on the record, and the arrangement is public domain with a fetched terms quote and a sha256, or engraved by this product. unknown, all-rights-reserved, no-redistribution, share-alike, and non-commercial are load refusals.
5. Training rows are emitted by the law. Each row carries the law version and the score hash. Train and held-out are disjoint song ids, committed before any row exists. The win bar is committed then too. A law bump that moves a label bumps the dataset in the same commit.
6. The host receives committed frames and may play them through an oscillator in slice 1. Samples and a singer are sockets. They consume committed note events. They are outside the hash. The singer is not in v1.

Proposed next slice: one re-fetched public-domain score, one admitted action, a golden hash native and under wasm, and an oscillator playback that is not hashed.
