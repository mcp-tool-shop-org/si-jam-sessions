A Rust score law, a host that only plays what that law already committed, and a training set that is a printout of those commitments. The sibling stays where it is. Nothing here is a repo yet.

si-jam-sessions would be built the way si-rpg-engine was. Phase 0 is a lock another family is invited to scar. The first code is a golden-hash harness, not a server. The model proposes a musical action. A checker that is not the model admits it. Replay is the seed plus the admitted-action log, and the model is not called during replay. Sound, samples, and a sung line are the picture. They are outside the hash.

The Rust knowledge base in readouts (catalog dated 2026-09-25, 230 of 230 recipes verified under rustc 1.98.1) is what makes the law shape non-negotiable. It has no music lane. Pitch, harmony, MIDI, and licenses are not in it. Those stay questions at the bottom.

The three owners

| Layer | Owner | What it is allowed to do |
|---|---|---|
| Law | A Rust crate, edition 2024, one wasm32-unknown-unknown cdylib | Hold the score as integer ticks. Admit or refuse an action. Hash a canonical snapshot. |
| Mind | A model, then the checker named for that action | Propose a note placement, a harmony label, or a reharmonization. Never write the score. |
| Picture | The host | Receive two committed score frames. Play them. Wear samples or a singer later, as a skin. |

Time inside the law is an integer tick at a fixed pulses-per-quarter, normalized once at ingest. The Rust KB's simulation lane is explicit: the law counts integer quanta, and the host owns the accumulator that turns those quanta into heard time. Swing, rubato, and a played take are picture. A 1/64-second physics quantum is the wrong import. A jam does not simulate contact. It admits edits to a score.

The snapshot is the KB's snapshot, applied to notes: little-endian fields in a declared order, a magic and a law version, collections sorted by a total key, no padding, no HashMap iteration, no ECS in the law. Pitch, onset, duration, velocity, and voice are integers, so the float lane never opens. sin and cos stay out of anything hashed. The file's sha256 is provenance. The hash is the parsed score.

Across the wasm boundary the KB is just as specific. Exports are extern "C" with #[unsafe(no_mangle)], which edition 2024 requires to be written that way. Every export returns a status code, because a panic is an unreachable trap. The host passes score bytes in. On wasm32-unknown-unknown, files, threads, and clocks are stubs. The law cannot open a song directory and it cannot own an audio device. wasm-opt does not run unless that exact step is pinned, because the digest is the bytes Cargo emitted. The digest is re-pinned only from an x86_64 Linux build. --locked, rust-toolchain.toml at 1.98.1, path remapping, and clippy -D warnings are the gate, in the same workflow file, not a second one.

Comes across: the provenance block (source URL, verbatim terms quote, sha256, title verdict, credited parties). The idea that a reharmonization is checkable by construction, with the melody locked and a non-triviality fraction signed before any training run. The preregistered win bar that withheld a victory at 12 of 16 when the bar was 13. One schema for ids and tempo, so two validators cannot disagree. Quarantine when the file's own title contradicts the catalog. A package with no samples in it. Stays in the sibling: the 84 uncleared songs, the four share-alike engravings, the eight reserved songs, the Salamander piano, the tract singer, the 49-tool surface, the frozen TypeScript chord snapshot, and the working-corpus-versus-published-subset split. Copying those MIDI bytes into a new tree would republish the problem under a new name.

Publish-friendly, as a load predicate

A song enters the law only when the composition is public domain in the United States and in the European Union, with the death year and the publication year written on the record, and the arrangement is either public domain with a fetched terms quote and a sha256, or engraved by this product and therefore under the product license.

unknown, all-rights-reserved, no-redistribution, and any share-alike or non-commercial term are refusals at ingest. They are not a status value a ready song is allowed to carry. There is no shadow corpus of the same pieces under a second license.

The ten public-domain library songs are the only borrowable fixtures, and only after a fresh fetch with a new receipt. They are not copied out of the sibling tree. Eight of the ten are one composer. A training set filtered down to them is a Joplin player. The corpus that can actually train is a set this product engraves: public-domain tunes, written in the law's own record, so the arrangement copyright is ours and the rows can ship under the product license. The split file is committed before the first engraving is labeled: train ids and held-out ids, disjoint, by song, never by phrase and never by genre. Held-out songs meet the same license predicate. Hiding the exam inside uncleared pop songs is how the last split happened.

Training, so the gates are in the first commit

The old gates arrived after the songs, the tools, and in one case after the DOI. Here the action language is the dataset schema, and it starts at five verbs: ingest a score, place notes, label a chord, reharmonize a span, refuse with a reason. An MCP server is a later picture of that language. Timeouts are a field on the verb, because a two-minute panel against a sixty-second client default was a product defect.

Every training row is emitted by the law from an admitted action. It carries law_version and the score hash. A person does not hand-author a trace that can drift from the score. A law change that moves a label moves the dataset version in the same commit. There is no sidecar snapshot of chord names to keep frozen while the function changes.

The execution gate runs in CI before any publish: every record's measure window exists in that score, the cited notes are the notes, and a row whose song would now be refused does not export. The win bar is a file next to the split, written before the run. Missing the bar is a recorded result. The checker for a reharmonization is the law's own predicate. A second model can sit on a calibration panel. It does not become the gate. That is the same scar the engine consults already marked.

Vocals stay a socket. The sibling's singer was paused because the mix was aligned to the file, not to the score events, against a 40 ms timing gate. The socket consumes committed note-on events. It does not get a v1, a landing page, or a local physical model of a throat.

What the first slice actually is

One public-domain score, re-fetched, receipt beside it. One admitted action. A golden hash of the snapshot, native and under wasm, written only by write-golden. The host plays that committed score through an oscillator so a person can hear the fixture. The audio is not hashed. No model, no MCP, no second song, no guitar, no singer.

That last sentence is the engine's own lesson, aimed at us. The engine's proposer seat froze because the world had nothing worth doing. A jam people cannot hear will freeze the same way. Slice 1 is allowed to make a sound. It is not allowed to make that sound part of the law.

This draft has the sibling's scars and the Rust KB. It has not had the citation-gated study-swarm that the engine's phase 0 had before anyone signed it. Signing it now would skip the step that changed the engine's clock.
