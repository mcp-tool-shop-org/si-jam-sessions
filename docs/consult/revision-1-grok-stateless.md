## 1. REVISED LOCK

A Rust score law, a host that only plays what that law already committed, and a training set that is a printout of those commitments. The sibling stays where it is. Nothing here is a repo yet.

si-jam-sessions would be built the way si-rpg-engine was. Phase 0 is a lock another family is invited to scar. The first code is a golden-hash harness, not a server. The model proposes a musical action. A checker that is not the model admits it. Replay is the seed plus the admitted-action log, and the model is not called during replay. Heard audio is the picture. It is outside the hash. A played take is not picture. Its integer onsets, pitches, velocities, note citations, and verdicts are law, because they are what gets graded and what a row can teach. The waveform stays picture.

The Rust knowledge base in readouts (catalog dated 2026-09-25, 230 of 230 recipes verified under rustc 1.98.1) is what makes the law shape non-negotiable. It has no music lane. Pitch, harmony, MIDI, and licenses are not in it. The binding rules used here are the wasm ABI lane, the simulation-architecture lane, the float lane by staying in integers, and the reproducible-build lane.

The three owners

| Layer | Owner | What it is allowed to do |
|---|---|---|
| Law | A Rust crate, edition 2024, one wasm32-unknown-unknown cdylib | Hold the score, the tempo map, and the take as integers. Step one tick whether or not a proposal arrives. Admit or refuse an action. Hash a canonical snapshot. |
| Mind | A model, then the checker named for that action | Propose a note placement, a harmony label, a reharmonization, or a typed claim. Never write the score. Never gate itself. |
| Picture | The host | Receive committed frames. Play them. Wear samples or a singer later, as a socket on committed note events. Own the audio callback. Decide nothing. |

Time inside the law is an integer tick at one fixed pulses-per-quarter, normalized once at ingest. An integer tempo map, in microseconds per quarter, is committed with the score. It sends ticks to samples by the floor of the exact rational. A take is integer sample onsets at one pinned rate, with integer pitch and velocity. Each take note cites a score note id or is marked as an addition. Swing, rubato, and a push or a pull are the integer difference between a take onset and the score onset mapped through that tempo map. They are not a second authored offset, because two writings of the same onset would let the hash disagree with itself.

The law steps one tick per step whether or not anything is proposed. It admits a proposal only when the onset is past a commit horizon measured in ticks, and it refuses a late proposal with the lateness in ticks. A committed tick does not change. That horizon is the jam law. A live jamming system that shows upcoming chords treats the span up to a commit time as no longer changeable, and leaves later predictions free to update. The horizon width is a named integer in the fixture, not a copied baseline. A late plan is a refusal. A cloud accompaniment search found that, in its remote-server setting, over 60 percent of configurations inside the search space violated the real-time conditions. Slice 1 therefore contains no model, and a model that arrives later sits beyond the horizon or not at all.

The host owns the accumulator that turns ticks into heard time, and it owns the callback that copies committed frames. A 128-sample audio buffer is not the law's step. A 1/64-second physics quantum remains the wrong import. A jam does not simulate contact. It admits edits, and it grades a take against the score.

The snapshot is the knowledge base's snapshot, applied to notes, the tempo map, the take, and the verdicts. Little-endian fields in a declared order, a magic and a law version, collections sorted by a total key, no padding, no HashMap iteration, no ECS in the law. Pitch, onset, duration, velocity, voice, microseconds per quarter, sample onset, and verdict are integers, so the float lane never opens. sin and cos stay out of anything hashed. The file's sha256 is provenance. The hash is the parsed score plus the parsed take and the parsed verdicts. The waveform is not in it.

Across the wasm boundary the rules do not move. Exports are extern "C" with #[unsafe(no_mangle)], which edition 2024 requires to be written that way. Every export returns a status code, because a panic is an unreachable trap. The host passes score bytes in. On wasm32-unknown-unknown, files, threads, and clocks are stubs. The law cannot open a song directory and it cannot own an audio device. wasm-opt does not run unless that exact step is pinned, because the digest is the bytes Cargo emitted. The digest is re-pinned only from an x86_64 Linux build. --locked, rust-toolchain.toml at 1.98.1, path remapping, and clippy -D warnings are the gate, in the same workflow file, not a second one. The sample rate, the grading gate in samples, and the commit horizon are named constants in that same file.

Publish-friendly, as a load predicate

A song enters the law only when the composition is public domain in the United States and in the European Union, with the death year and the publication year written on the record, and the arrangement is either public domain with a fetched terms quote and a sha256, engraved by this product and therefore under the product license, or CC-BY-4.0 in its own tier. On 1 January 2026, published works from 1930 entered the US public domain. That page is about US law only. A publication year is necessary and not sufficient. A missing year is a refusal. The record also carries the source edition, the publisher, and the edition year.

German law protects a scientific edition of a work or text that is no longer in copyright, when the edition is the result of scientifically organised activity and differs substantially from previously known editions. That right expires 25 years after publication, or 25 years after production if the edition was not released in that period. This lock is not a ruling on substantial difference, and it is not legal advice. An edition whose year is missing, or whose 25-year term has not run, is refused, because the EU public-domain predicate is then not met. A person confirms the years before a retry. The machine does not publish through the gap.

The in-file licence must equal the host page's licence. Both quotes and both sha256 values sit on the receipt. A mismatch is a refusal, not a choice of the more public label. One public-domain MusicXML record later found that 31,221 of its songs, 12.29 percent, disagreed with the page, and it recommends a conflict-free subset of 222,856 files. That record chose the public-facing label. This lock refuses the file instead. The subset is a candidate pool, admitted file by file with a fresh receipt, never copied in as a tree. The record itself is CC-BY-4.0, so a bulk copy would also skip the edition test.

A retrieved project page states that its scores are released under CC0 and describes a lieder corpus of over 1,300 songs, plus a string-quartet collection that the same page does not count. That shelf is larger than the sibling's ten public-domain engravings. It is still a candidate pool. Each file needs its own receipt, and the edition field is recovered per file. Counts that were not on the page are not in this lock.

unknown, all-rights-reserved, no-redistribution, any share-alike term, any non-commercial term, and any no-derivatives term are refusals at ingest. They are not a status value a ready song is allowed to carry. A source term that forbids training is a refusal even when other reuse is allowed. There is no shadow corpus of the same piece under a second license. A piece engraved here does not also enter as CC-BY.

CC-BY-4.0 enters a separate tier. The row carries a credit-ledger id. The ledger and the model card carry the creator names and a link to each source. That is the attribution a conservative reading of the licence primer allows for training: a link to the dataset source, and, where a particular work can be tied to an output, a link to that work. Share-alike stays refused. The same primer says that if models or outputs based on ShareAlike content are shared publicly, following that condition means the same licence. Rows in the CC-BY tier may be published beside an MIT or Apache crate. They are not mixed into a public-domain claim. The ten public-domain library songs remain the only borrowable fixtures from the sibling, and only after a fresh fetch with a new receipt. They are not copied out of the sibling tree. Eight of the ten are one composer. A training set filtered down to them is a Joplin player.

The corpus that can actually train is a set this product engraves, plus candidate files that survive the predicate one by one: public-domain tunes, written in the law's own record, so the arrangement copyright is ours, and CC-BY-4.0 rows that carry the ledger. The split file is committed before the first engraving is labeled: train ids and held-out ids, disjoint, by work, never by phrase and never by genre. Each id carries its tier. Held-out songs meet the same licence predicate. Hiding the exam inside uncleared pop songs is how the last split happened.

Training, so the gates are in the first commit

The action language is the dataset schema, and it starts at five verbs: ingest a score, place notes, label a chord, reharmonize a span, refuse with a reason. A teaching sentence is not a sixth verb. Typed claims inside it, a bar, a label, a function, are admitted by the checker named for that claim. The sentence is rendered from those claims by template. Free text is picture. It is never a proposal, and it is never hashed. An MCP server is a later picture of the five verbs. Timeouts are a field on the verb.

Every class is a hand-authored predicate or a refusal. No model is a gate. A second model may sit on a calibration panel. It does not become the gate.

Ingest admits only when the licence predicate holds, the parse is integers, a canonical re-encode round-trips, the file's own title events match the catalog, and the in-file licence equals the page. Otherwise the file is quarantined or refused. Placing notes admits only inside instrument range, with no overlap inside a voice, with the onset past the commit horizon, and with each note citing a score note or marked as a declared addition. A chord label is a predicate over the law's pitch-class analysis. Equivalent spellings are all admitted. None is trained as the only gold. A reharmonization admits only when the melody bytes are unchanged, the consonance rule holds, the signed non-triviality fraction holds, and a one-bar copy check against the whole training corpus fails to find a copy. The copy check is a lock rule. It is not a citation of a detector this revision did not open. An edit distance on chord roots is not the checker.

Every training row is emitted by the law from an admitted action. It carries law_version and the score hash, and it is admitted only if the law replays it to the same hash. A person does not hand-author a trace that can drift from the score. A law change that moves a label moves the dataset version in the same commit. There is no sidecar snapshot of chord names to keep frozen while the function changes.

The execution gate runs in CI before any publish: every record's measure window exists in that score, the cited notes are the notes, the take verdicts recompute, and a row whose song would now be refused does not export. The win bar is a file next to the split, written before the run. Missing the bar is a recorded result. Any of the three owners can halt a publish when a hash mismatches, a receipt is missing, a row's song would now be refused, or a compensator in the table below has no owner. The halt is a recorded refusal, not a silent skip. Publish tokens never enter the law. Uncleared bytes never enter a row.

Vocals stay a socket, and they are not in v1. The socket is not a refusal of the product. It is a refusal to let a singer become state. Every committed note-on carries a stable note_id and an onset_sample at the law's rate. Score notes derive onset_sample through the tempo map. Take notes are admitted directly. A later singer consumes note_id, onset_sample, duration, and pitch, and nothing else. The grading gate is 1,920 samples, which is 40 ms at the pinned rate of 48 kHz. A tick alone is not enough, because the tempo map decides when a syllable sounds.

What the first slice actually is

One public-domain score, re-fetched, receipt beside it, edition year on the record. One constructed take of that score, admitted as integer sample onsets: three notes late, one note early, one wrong pitch, with the lateness written in samples in the fixture file. The law steps in ticks, maps through the tempo map, and grades every take note against its score note inside a two-sided 1,920-sample gate at exact pitch. It writes one row per note. The target states the comparison in digits.

A golden hash covers the score, the tempo map, the take, and the verdicts, native and under wasm, written only by write-golden. The host plays two oscillator voices and a click from committed beat events, so a person hears the late notes against the beat. The waveform is not hashed. A live key uses the same admit path and is heard back on that path. It is not inside the golden, because a golden that depends on a live clock is not a golden. No model, no MCP, no second song, no guitar, no singer, no sample library, no drum loop.

An oscillator on a fixed score, alone, is a music box. The constructed take is the thing a person can hear as late or wrong. The live key is the thing a person can do. The hash stays on the constructed fixture.

## 3. REJECTIONS ON EVIDENCE

The 128-sample law step does not rest on an opened source. The review marked it medium and unchecked. It is a design judgement: the host already owns the accumulator, and an audio buffer inside the law would open a second clock beside the tick.

The OpenScore counts of about 1,500 lieder and about 200 quartets are outweighed by the page retrieved here, which says over 1,300 songs and does not count the quartets. The CC0 statement on that page is kept. The counts are not.

The public-domain MusicXML record recommends proceeding with the website licence and using its conflict-free subset. What outweighs following that procedure is the same record: it documents 31,221 disagreements, and this lock already refuses a contradictory term rather than picking a label. File-by-file receipt still applies to the subset. Bulk ingest is a design judgement on top of that evidence, because the record's own licence is CC-BY-4.0 and a tree copy would skip the edition year.

The Levenshtein root check does not rest on an opened source. Rejecting it is a design judgement. The checker stays the melody lock, the consonance rule, and the signed fraction.

The demand for several thousand pieces before any preregistered run does not rest on an opened source. The floor of 36 is the sibling cohort that settled the power question, not a claim that thousands are required before a run exists.

A 100 ms response claim and a universal four-beat plan were in the dispatch. The re-read of the jamming paper found a commit time, and a user study whose lookahead was always four beats and whose commit setting varied. It did not find a 100 ms result in the sections searched. Those constants are not in the lock. The principle that a committed span does not change is in the lock.

The delay summary's figure, delays under 11.5 ms, is a clapping-pair result on a page whose journal PDF was not opened. The review already marked it as not a keyboard result. Nothing opened here turns it into a law constant. The grading gate stays the sibling's 40 ms, pinned in samples. That is a design judgement informed by the limit the summary itself states.

A drum-sample library was not rejected by an opened licence. No licence was opened, so none is picked. The click is generated from committed beat events.

## 4. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE DIRECTOR

You probably expect the load predicate to stay public-domain or self-engraved only. The lock admits CC-BY-4.0 in a separate tier with a credit ledger, because both reviews asked for it and the primer treats a source link as training attribution, while share-alike, non-commercial, and no-derivatives stay refused. Override by striking the tier and leaving those rows uningested.

You probably expect swing and rubato to stay in the host. The lock hashes integer take onsets and verdicts, and leaves the waveform outside the hash, because a score-only row cannot teach the timing judgement both reviews said was missing. Override by moving onset_sample and the verdicts back to the host and accepting that rows will not teach timing.

You probably expect the old 13 of 16 bar to remain the win line. The lock refuses a preregistered claim below 36 paired items from at least 10 works by at least 5 composers, because the 12 of 16 miss was later a power problem and the same adapters won 29 of 36. Override by writing a different floor into the win-bar file before any row exists.

You probably expect the German 25-year edition term to be applied only after someone decides the edition is scientific and substantially different. The lock refuses any edition whose year is missing or whose 25 years have not run, which will refuse editions the statute might not cover. Override by replacing that refusal with a named human clearance for editions inside the term.

You probably expect a live keyboard take inside the first golden hash, so the harness is a jam before it is a fixture. The lock hashes a constructed take so the golden does not depend on a live clock, and puts the live key on the same admit path outside the golden. Override by requiring one recorded live take, frozen as a fixture, before write-golden runs.
