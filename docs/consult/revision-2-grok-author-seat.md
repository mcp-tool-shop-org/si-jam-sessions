# Author-seat revision — Cursor Grok (2026-09-25)

Author seat of record for the si-jam-sessions phase-0 lock. Worked from the six locked items, the draft, and the two consult replies; did not have candidate draft v1 (OpenRouter) or the advisor's review of it. Recorded verbatim as delivered to the Director.

---

The revised lock is below. Integers stay in the hash, the snapshot stays byte-stable, and the licence refusals stay. The take, the step, and the click are now in the law. The waveform stays out.

## Locked shape

1. The law is Rust, edition 2024, rustc 1.98.1, one wasm cdylib with a raw C ABI. Status codes cross the boundary. Panics do not. The host passes bytes in. The law does not read the disk and does not own an audio device.
2. A score is integer ticks at one fixed pulses-per-quarter, normalized at ingest. A take is integer sample onsets at 48 kHz with integer pitch and velocity; each take note cites a score note id or is marked as an addition. An integer tempo map (microseconds per quarter) sends ticks to samples by the floor of the exact rational. The law steps one quantum at a time whether or not anything is proposed. That quantum is a fixed integer sample count declared in the law version. The law admits a proposal only past a commit horizon of an integer number of quanta, and refuses a late proposal with its lateness in quanta. Pitch, onset, duration, velocity, and voice are integers. The hashed snapshot is little-endian, versioned, sorted, and free of `HashMap` order. Replay is the seed plus the admitted-action log. The model is not called during replay.
3. Five verbs: ingest, place notes, label a chord, reharmonize a span, refuse with a reason. The reharmonization checker is a hand-authored predicate: melody locked, a consonance rule, a non-triviality fraction signed before any training run. A teaching sentence is not a verb. Free text is never a proposal. Typed claims use the verbs above. Any sentence shown to a person is picture.
4. A song is ingested only when the composition is public domain in the US and the EU, with years on the record, and the arrangement is public domain with a fetched terms quote and a sha256, or engraved by this product. The source edition, publisher, and year are on the record. A third-party scholarly edition still inside its edition term is refused. An engraving this product makes is the product's own arrangement, under the product licence. The in-file licence must equal the host page's licence, or the file is refused. `unknown`, all-rights-reserved, no-redistribution, share-alike, and non-commercial are load refusals. CC-BY-4.0 enters a separate tier whose rows carry a credit-ledger id; the model card links each such source.
5. Training rows are emitted by the law. Each row carries the law version and the score hash. Train and held-out are disjoint song ids, committed before any row exists. The win bar is committed then too. A law bump that moves a label bumps the dataset in the same commit.
6. The host receives committed frames and, in slice 1, plays them through an oscillator together with a click taken from committed beat events. Samples and a singer are sockets. Every committed note-on carries `note_id` and `onset_sample`. Sockets consume only these. They are outside the hash. The singer is not in v1, so the clock exists before a singer attaches. The waveform is not hashed.

Proposed next slice: one re-fetched public-domain score with its receipt, and one constructed take admitted as integer sample onsets (three notes late by 30, 45, and 60 ms, one early by 45 ms, one wrong pitch). The law grades every note against its score note on a two-sided 1,920-sample gate at exact pitch, and writes one row per note whose target states the comparison in digits. One golden hash covers the score, the take, and the verdicts, native and under wasm. The host plays two oscillator voices plus the click, so the late notes are audible. A live keyboard take uses the same admit path. The waveform is not hashed. No model, no MCP.

## Ground truth, corrected

Measured on `ai-jam-sessions` at `b015f1d`. The live tool surface in `README.md` is **54 tools** and 4 prompt templates. The "49 tools" figure is the historical v2.3.0 sentence in that same file, not the commit's surface.

The 12/16 miss against a 13/16 bar stands as the v1 result. It was later settled: `docs/finetune-arc-v2-b1-eval-report.md` records a preregistered 36-record cohort, **29/36** paired wins (2 ties, bar 24/34), and calls the 12/16 miss a power artifact. That report is not a reason to invent a new bar in this lock.

The vocals-paused claim is unconfirmed. `README.md` line 563 at v2.3.0 describes a score clock that derives each syllable from the song's MIDI, with a timing gate of 40 ms. 1,920 samples is that gate at 48 kHz.
