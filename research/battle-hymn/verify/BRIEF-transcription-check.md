# Check a transcription against its source scans

You are an independent checker from a different model family than the transcriber. Read-only.

The file below is an ABC transcription of an 1862 sheet-music edition, "Battle Hymn of the Republic" (Boston:
Oliver Ditson & Co., plate 21454). The three page images that follow are that edition's pages 3, 4 and 5, in
order. Tune X:1 is pages 3–4 (a three-bar piano introduction, verse 1, and the chorus); tune X:2 is page 5 (the
music for verses 2–5). Each bar in the file carries a comment giving its page, system and position. `L:1/16`, so
lengths are in sixteenths; `M:C`.

The chord symbols (voice CH) are the transcriber's reading, not printed in the edition; check them only for
consistency with the printed notes. Check everything else against the images: every pitch (with its octave,
clef and key signature), every rhythm, rests, ties, accidentals, the upbeats, bar counts, and repeats.

Report, bar by bar, only discrepancies:

```
DISCREPANCIES:
- X:<tune> bar <n>, voice <V>: transcription says <...>; the scan shows <...> (page, system)
UNREADABLE: bars or places where the image does not let you decide, and why
VERDICT: matches | matches with the discrepancies listed | does not match
```

Say "none" under DISCREPANCIES if every checked bar matches. Do not guess where the image is unclear; list it
under UNREADABLE.
